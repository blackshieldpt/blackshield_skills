# Rollout plan: new billing page

We are going to roll out the new billing page to customers over the next few weeks,
starting with some internal users and then moving on to a small group of customers
once things look okay, and after that everyone, assuming nothing bad happens.

## Phases

1. Internal staff (week 1). Support will keep an eye on it.
2. Five percent of customers (week 2), picked at random.
3. Everyone (week 3 or 4, depending).

## Rollback

If something goes wrong we will turn it off. The on-call engineer decides.

## Communication

We will tell customers about it at some point, probably by email, before phase 3.
